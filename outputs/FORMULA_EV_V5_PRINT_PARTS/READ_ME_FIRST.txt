FORMULA EV V5 — 55 PRINTABLE STL PARTS

This folder contains every physical part instance required for one complete model. Four tires, four rims, four brake rotors and four center locks are already included as separate files. Wheel parts are oriented flat.

MATERIALS
- Print files containing “slick_tire” in TPU 95A.
- Print all other files in PETG.
- Optional: use 2 mm metal rods for wheel axles and active-wing hinge pins.

STARTING SETTINGS
0.15-0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 20-30% gyroid infill. Enable build-plate supports for suspension, wing structures, halo and linkages.

IMPORTANT
Print in several batches. Do not independently rescale any part. Print and test one moving-wing flap and hinge area before committing to the full set. These meshes were derived from the final visual V5 design; very small details may require a 0.25 mm nozzle or local reinforcement in your slicer.

Use PARTS_AND_MATERIALS.csv as the checklist.
