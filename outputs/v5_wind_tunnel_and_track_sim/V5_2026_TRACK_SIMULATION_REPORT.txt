MODEL LIMITATIONS
The model uses track length, circuit category and assumed average speed calibrated to modern formula-car pace. It does not solve a racing line, braking zones, tire temperatures, battery state, motor torque curve, gear ratios, elevation, weather, traffic, safety cars or strategy. Typical uncertainty is about +/-2.5% for qualifying and +/-4% for race pace; Madrid is less certain because it is new. An electric car sustaining these laps also requires an energy-storage and thermal model that is not yet defined.
