FORMULA EV V3 — 2022-2025 GROUND-EFFECT FLOOR STUDY

This is an original educational scale interpretation of publicly documented 2022-2025 Formula 1 ground-effect concepts, not a scan or exact copy of any team car.

SYSTEMS MODELED
- Twin Venturi inlets, descending ramps, narrow throats and progressive rear expansion
- Four inlet fences per side
- Raised floor-edge ramp and curl
- Floor-edge vortex-generator row and rear cut-in wing
- Central plank with inspection holes and separate titanium skid set
- Bib/tea-tray element and floor stays
- Left/right multi-stage diffuser shells
- Six diffuser strakes and narrowed boat-tail center keel
- Rear-wheel wake/tire-squirt fences

FILES
FORMULA_EV_V3_COMPLETE_2022_2025_FLOOR.glb: whole car with animated DRS.
FORMULA_EV_V3_COMPLETE_UNDERFLOOR.glb: floor system only, assembled.
FORMULA_EV_V3_EXPLODED_UNDERFLOOR.glb: floor elements separated for study.
Numbered GLBs: every major car and floor component individually.
