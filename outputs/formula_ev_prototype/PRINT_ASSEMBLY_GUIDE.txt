FORMULA EV PROTOTYPE — PRINT AND ASSEMBLY GUIDE

A completely new open-wheel electric formula-car study model. Overall assembled envelope: approximately 210 x 153 x 65 mm.

PRINT MATERIALS
PETG or PLA+ for body, wings, floor, suspension and rims. TPU 95A for slick tires. Transparent or colored PETG may be used for display accents.

SETTINGS
0.20 mm layer height, 4 walls, 20-25% gyroid infill. Use build-plate supports for wings, halo and suspension. Print the monocoque and floor separately.

ASSEMBLY
1. Glue battery, motor and inverter to the floor/chassis before fitting the monocoque.
2. Fit sidepods, cockpit and halo.
3. Install front and rear suspension modules at x=-68 and x=67 mm.
4. Assemble each wheel: rotor, rim, TPU tire and center-lock nut. Glue only the center-lock nut to the axle tip.
5. Attach nose, front wing and rear wing last.

The assembled STL is intended for viewing and static printing. Use separate STLs for functional rotating wheels and clearer multi-material printing. This is an educational scale prototype, not a road-safe design.
