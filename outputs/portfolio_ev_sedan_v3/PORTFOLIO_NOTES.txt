EV SEDAN V3 — COLLEGE PORTFOLIO NOTES

DESIGN INTENT
A printable electric performance sedan study combining industrial design, mechanical packaging and design-for-additive-manufacturing. The exterior is a low fastback with functional aero cues; the technical model includes a skateboard battery, rear e-axle motor and reduction gearbox, power electronics, suspension, steering rack, brakes and a four-seat interior.

ENGINEERING STORY
- Packaging: flat battery pack low between axles; e-axle and inverter positioned near rear drive unit.
- Dynamics: low center of gravity, wide track, double-wishbone-style arms and visible brake rotors.
- Manufacturing: body split into printable modules; TPU tires and rigid PETG structural parts; alignment pegs and wheel retainers simplify assembly.
- Iteration: evolved from a bare skateboard chassis to a sedan, then to a modular suspension model and finally a system-level portfolio prototype.

IMPORTANT
This is an educational scale prototype, not a road-safe vehicle design. Validate tolerances and motor temperatures before powered testing.
