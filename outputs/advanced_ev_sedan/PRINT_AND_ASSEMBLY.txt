ADVANCED EV SEDAN PRINTING KIT

PARTS
1x advanced_body.stl
1x fastback_cabin.stl
1x rear_wing.stl
2x suspension_axle_module_x2.stl
4x sport_wheel_x4.stl
4x wheel_retainer_cap_x4.stl

Recommended material: PETG. Use TPU only for optional tires if you later separate the tire geometry.
Layer height: 0.20 mm; walls: 4; infill: 20-25%; supports: build plate only for suspension modules and wing.

ASSEMBLY
1. Glue cabin to the four deck pegs.
2. Glue axle-module bridges into the underside mounting areas at x = +/-65 mm. Keep arms free.
3. Slide wheels onto 7.6 mm diameter axle stubs; gently sand bores if needed.
4. Glue retainer caps only to axle ends; do not glue wheels.
5. Glue wing on the rear deck.
6. Install a 130-size DC motor or N20 gearmotor in the front cradle; final drivetrain coupling depends on your motor/gearbox.

Overall assembled envelope: about 192 x 101 x 59 mm. Fits a 220 x 220 x 250 mm printer.
The 3MF contains the entire kit as individually selectable objects.
