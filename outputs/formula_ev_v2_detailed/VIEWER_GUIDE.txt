FORMULA EV V2 GLB VIEWER GUIDE

Open FORMULA_EV_V2_COMPLETE_ANIMATED_DRS.glb in Blender for the best inspection. The model has separately named components. Select and hide body parts to inspect suspension, battery, motor and cooling systems.

DRS ANIMATION
In Blender, switch to the Animation workspace and press Play. The rear flap opens to 28 degrees and closes over a 3-second loop. If a viewer does not support glTF animations, the flap remains visible as a separate selectable object.

INDIVIDUAL FILES
The numbered GLBs contain each major system by itself, centered at the origin for easy rotation and inspection. Wheel component files are intended to be reused four times.
