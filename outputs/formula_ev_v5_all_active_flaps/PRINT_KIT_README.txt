FORMULA EV V5 — COMPLETE 55-PART PRINT KIT

The 3MF contains all 55 physical part instances as separately selectable objects. Repeated wheel parts are included four times. Wheel parts are already rotated flat.

IMPORTANT: The complete layout occupies approximately 600 x 594 mm and therefore cannot print on one 220 x 220 mm plate. In Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer, use Auto Arrange and distribute the objects over multiple plates/batches. Do not scale parts independently.

MATERIAL PLAN
PETG: body, aero, suspension, rims, floor and mechanical components.
TPU 95A: four slick tires only.
Optional metal: 2 mm rods for reliable wing hinges and wheel axles.

The colored file carries display/material assignments. If your slicer rejects material extensions, use the compatibility file and assign filaments manually. The GLB was a presentation model; test-fit small joints before printing the whole kit.
