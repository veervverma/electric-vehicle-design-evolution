FORMULA EV V4 — DUAL ACTIVE AERO

The complete GLB contains two independent glTF animations:
1. REAR DRS OPEN CLOSE: rear flap rotates +28 degrees.
2. FRONT ACTIVE AERO LOW DRAG: left and right front flaps rotate -14 degrees together.

In Blender, import the GLB, open the Animation workspace, select an animation/action and press Play. Some basic GLB viewers display the components but do not play multiple animation tracks.

The front active-aero design is an educational concept. Current 2022-2025 Formula 1 regulations do not use this exact active front-wing arrangement.

All major car, underfloor and active-aero systems are provided as individual numbered GLBs.
