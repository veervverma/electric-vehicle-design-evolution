What the three references contributed

1 · Separation and stall

A wing-angle experiment lets you inspect computed vorticity and reverse flow. Thin-airfoil lift trends only describe attached flow; they do not predict stall. No fixed stall threshold is imposed. Attached-flow theory: Cₗ ≈ 2π(α − α₀), with angles in radians; this formula does not predict stall.
