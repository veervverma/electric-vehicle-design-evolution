How to interpret it

This is an educational 2D experiment at a chosen reduced Reynolds number, with selectable independently rasterized grids. A side projection merges parts that sit at different depths and cannot represent 3D underfloor flow. No full-scale downforce or drag is inferred. Top and road rows impose freestream velocity; the outlet uses zero-gradient extrapolation.
