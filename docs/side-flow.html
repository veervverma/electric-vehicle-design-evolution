Flow controls

Particles are restricted to the lower half: car and wake. The upper velocity color field is retained.

Why a smooth wake? Default Re ≈ 182, a coarse 2D projection and numerical diffusion limit wake detail. At default speed, one car-length flow time takes ≈4,050 solver steps. Allow several flow times. Vorticity reveals shear and rotation; it does not by itself prove an eddy. This view does not resolve a real F1 car’s turbulent 3D wake.

What is being solved?

A live D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann calculation approximates low-Mach Navier–Stokes flow. White tracers follow the computed velocity; colors show speed, vorticity, or relative pressure. The projected car uses the supplied W11 mesh.

How to interpret it

This is an educational 2D experiment at reduced Reynolds number. A side projection merges parts that sit at different depths and cannot represent 3D underfloor flow. No full-scale downforce or drag is inferred. Top and road rows impose freestream velocity; the outlet uses zero-gradient extrapolation.
